01 / Interiors

Selected Interiors

A set of interior spaces from recent studio work — material studies in stone, timber, and light, shown alongside the architectural case studies below.

01 / Studio

The Glasshouse Studio

A drafting studio held above the ground on a grid of slender steel columns. Full-height glazing turns the walls into instruments for daylight, fading from a reflective skin outside to clear glass the moment you step in.

  • 220 M² GFA
  • STEEL + GLASS
  • COMPLETED 2025
02 / Residence

Courtyard House

Two timber-clad volumes face each other across a still reflecting pool. Every living space opens fully onto the water, so the courtyard — not a hallway — becomes the route through the house.

  • 260 M² GFA
  • TIMBER + GLASS
  • COMPLETED 2025
03 / Pavilion

Cantilever Pavilion

A single concrete core carries two glazed volumes out in opposite directions, stacked and rotated ninety degrees from one another, floating over a dark reflecting plinth.

  • 180 M² GFA
  • CONCRETE + GLASS
  • COMPLETED 2026
